# Retail Analytics Project – Presentation

## 📌 Overview

This folder contains the final presentation for the **Retail Data Analytics Project**.

The presentation walks through the complete analytics pipeline — starting from raw retail data, moving through SQL-based transformation and modeling, and concluding with interactive Power BI dashboards and key business insights.

It is designed to simulate a real-world business presentation to stakeholders.

---

## 🎯 Presentation Objective

The goal of this presentation is to:

* Demonstrate the end-to-end retail analytics workflow
* Showcase SQL-based data modeling (360 tables)
* Present key business insights derived from the data
* Visualize insights through interactive Power BI dashboards
* Communicate findings in a structured, business-friendly manner

---

## 🗂 Presentation Structure

### 1️⃣ Problem Statement & Business Context

* Overview of retail dataset
* Business objectives
* Key analytical questions:

  * Who are our most valuable customers?
  * Which stores are performing best?
  * What products drive revenue?
  * How are orders distributed over time?

---

### 2️⃣ Data Preparation & SQL Modeling

* Data inspection and cleaning
* Transformation of raw data into structured analytical tables
* Creation of:

  * **Customer 360**
  * **Store 360**
  * **Order 360**

This step reflects a real-world data engineering + analytics workflow.

---

### 3️⃣ Customer 360 Insights

* Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
* Purchase frequency & recency
* Customer segmentation potential
* High-value customer identification

**Business Impact:**
Enables targeted marketing and retention strategies.

---

### 4️⃣ Store 360 Insights

* Store-level revenue performance
* Order volume comparison
* KPI tracking

**Business Impact:**
Helps identify top-performing stores and underperforming locations.

---

### 5️⃣ Order & Product Performance

* Revenue trends over time
* Basket size analysis
* Category-level performance
* Product contribution to total revenue

**Business Impact:**
Supports pricing strategy and product optimization decisions.

---

### 6️⃣ Customer Satisfaction Analysis

* Satisfaction score trends
* Correlation between satisfaction and purchase behavior

**Business Impact:**
Improves customer retention strategy and service optimization.

---

### 7️⃣ Dashboard Demonstration

The presentation includes visuals from the Power BI dashboard covering:

* Executive overview
* Customer analytics
* Store performance
* Product performance
* Order analysis
* Customer satisfaction metrics
