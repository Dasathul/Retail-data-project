# Retail Analytics Dashboard Suite

This folder contains a complete set of business intelligence dashboards built using **Power BI**.
The dashboards are powered by SQL-derived analytical 360 tables and are designed to provide actionable business insights across customers, orders, products, and stores.

---

# 🎯 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard suite is divided into six analytical views:

---

## 1️⃣ Executive Dashboard

**Purpose:** High-level business performance monitoring.

Key Metrics:

* Total Revenue
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Total Orders
* Total Customers

Key Insights:

* Revenue and profit trends over time
* Profit contribution by state
* Revenue by product category
* Impact of discounts on profitability

This dashboard provides a CEO-level overview for strategic decision-making.

---

## 2️⃣ Customer360 Dashboard

**Purpose:** Deep dive into customer behavior and segmentation.

Key Metrics:

* Total Customers
* Average Profit per Customer
* Average Spend
* Average Rating
* % Discounted Orders

Analysis Includes:

* Profit by payment type
* Gender-wise spending
* Monetary segmentation (High / Mid / Low value customers)
* Recency segmentation (Recent / Moderate / Dormant customers)
* Channel-wise customer distribution

This dashboard supports customer targeting and retention strategy.

---

## 3️⃣ Order360 Dashboard

**Purpose:** Order-level performance analysis.

Key Metrics:

* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Profit Margin
* Total Orders
* Average Quantity
* Average Rating

Analysis Includes:

* Revenue trends over time
* Revenue by channel
* AOV comparison across channels
* Profit vs Quantity relationship
* Category-wise customer distribution

This dashboard helps optimize order profitability and channel performance.

---

## 4️⃣ Product Performance Dashboard

**Purpose:** Category and product-level performance evaluation.

Highlights:

* Highest Revenue Category
* Highest Profit Category
* Highest Rated Category

Analysis Includes:

* Average rating by category
* Discount distribution by monetary segment
* Profit contribution by recency segment
* Year-wise profit comparison of top categories

This dashboard supports inventory and category management decisions.

---

## 5️⃣ Store360 Dashboard

**Purpose:** Store-level revenue and profitability tracking.

Key Metrics:

* Best Performing Store
* AOV by Store
* Total Revenue
* Average Profit
* Average Rating

Analysis Includes:

* Location-wise revenue, profit, and discount
* Customer distribution by state
* Revenue split between weekday and weekend
* Category-wise revenue and discount performance

This dashboard enables operational performance monitoring.

---

## 6️⃣ Customer Satisfaction Dashboard

**Purpose:** Customer experience and satisfaction analysis.

Key Metrics:

* Satisfied, Neutral, and Not Satisfied customers
* Overall Rating
* Revenue by satisfaction category
* Discount trends by satisfaction segment

Analysis Includes:

* Satisfaction trends over time
* Rating vs Profit relationship
* Channel-wise satisfaction breakdown

This dashboard connects customer experience to business profitability.

---

# 🧠 Business Value

Together, these dashboards provide:

* End-to-end retail performance monitoring
* Customer segmentation and targeting insights
* Profitability analysis across stores and channels
* Product-level strategic intelligence
* Data-driven decision support for management

This project demonstrates full-cycle analytics:
Raw Data → SQL Transformation → 360 Modeling → Business Intelligence Visualization.
